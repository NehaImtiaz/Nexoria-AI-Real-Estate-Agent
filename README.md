# 🏢 Nexoria – Global AI Real Estate Sales Agent

An enterprise-grade, 24/7 AI Sales Agent engineered for **Pakistani & UK Real Estate Agencies**. Nexoria automates property discovery, qualifies international and local leads, handles multi-lingual/cultural interactions (English & Roman Urdu), and instantly synchronizes site visits directly into Google Calendar in real-time.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Multi-Market Property Matching:** 
  - **Pakistan Market:** Full coverage of major hubs (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad) with PKR pricing and local property taxonomy.
  - **UK Market:** Seamless handling of UK property listings (London, Manchester, Birmingham) with GBP (£) pricing, freehold/leasehold queries, and UK terminology.
- **Bilingual & Multi-Dialect NLP:** 
  - Fluent in **English** (UK Buyers & Expat Investors) and **Roman Urdu** (Local Pakistani Clients & Overseas Pakistani Diaspora).
- **Frictionless Lead Capture:** 
  - Automatically extracts Client Name, Phone/WhatsApp Number, Site Visit Timing, and Preferred Callback Slots.
- **Automated Google Calendar Synchronization:** 
  - Converts inquiries into confirmed calendar events with complete lead metadata automatically populated into the event details.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Core Engine:** Botpress Studio (Vibe Engine)
- **Integrations:** Google Calendar API (OAuth 2.0)
- **Deployment Channels:** Webchat Widget, WhatsApp Integration Ready
- **Knowledge Base:** Custom Dual-Market Property Inventory (PK & UK listings)
